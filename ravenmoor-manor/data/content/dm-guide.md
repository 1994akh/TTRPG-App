# DM Guide (DM eyes only)

## The solution in sixty seconds

**Who:** Dr. Thaddeus Quill, the family physician. **How:** he poisoned Silas's 7:45 tonic with Banshee's Breath, used the secret passage to enter the locked study and steal the new will, then played the Lantern Widow in a gown, veil and glowing foxfire gloves. **Why:** he secretly owns Edric's debts. If the new will vanishes, Edric inherits the manor, Quill seizes it, and the moonsilver under the crypt is his.

The will is in the false bottom of Quill's medical bag. Every other suspect is guilty of something harmless: Morwenna is eloping, Edric is broke, Pip faked earlier ghosts, Bramwell keeps ghost-warding rituals, and Mrs. Crane left the lilies.

## Prep timeline

About two hours of prep in total, most of it a week out.

**A week before**

- [ ] Read the Adventure journal once, start to finish
- [ ] Check the showdown actors: Quill, the scarecrow and the skeleton (the social NPCs are optional)
- [ ] Order or pick up props, especially the plague doctor mask
- [ ] Ask players to bring level 5 characters and think up a costume for them

**The day before**

- [ ] Print any paper handouts and the DM screen you want at the table
- [ ] Seal the will scroll and Edric's letter (if using physical props)
- [ ] Read Act 1 aloud once to find your pace, and practice Quill's voice
- [ ] Open the crypt and check the showdown tokens and the Midnight Bell lair actions

**Thirty minutes before**

- [ ] Set up the DM screen with the solution panel facing you
- [ ] Lay out the twelve tealights (or open the Twelve Candles clock), candy, and the doctor's bag by your seat
- [ ] Cue the ambience playlist
- [ ] Put the plague doctor mask where players can't see it

## If the players do this, you do that

Whatever the players try, keep the case solvable and the clock moving; never block a good idea, just make it cost a candle.

| If the players... | You... |
|---|---|
| Accuse someone before candle 6 | Let Quill agree warmly. Run the next escalation while the accused is under guard, which proves them wrong. |
| Want to search Quill or his bag | He refuses indignantly: "Patient records, sir!" A forced search or Sleight of Hand (DC 15) finds the will, and the finale starts early (Adventure, Act 2). |
| Try to leave or send for help | The road is flooded and the ferry won't cross until Fennick does at midnight. Bramwell warns them off the pond path. |
| Attack or threaten a suspect | Bramwell steps in with his shovel and the household goes cold. The frightened suspect blurts out their secret, not a confession. |
| Fixate on one innocent suspect | Let the evidence fail to stick. Have that suspect volunteer their ★ clue, which points somewhere else. |
| Watch Quill all night | He slips away during a scene change. If they truly never let him go, he bolts with a smoke flask at the midnight bell. |
| Split into many groups | One candle per round of scenes, about 5 real minutes per group, and cut away on cliffhangers. |
| Run long | Merge Escalations 2 and 3, have Pip piece it together aloud, and snuff two candles per scene. |
| Finish early | Stretch the epilogue: Fennick reads every bequest, and the party can explore the moonsilver in the crypt. |
| Leave one player quiet | Give them an NPC who will only confide in them. Pip picks the shyest character. |

## Rulings for investigation spells and abilities

Level 5 characters have real tools; let them work, because a clue that lands fast leaves more time for the fun parts.

| Spell or ability | What it reveals |
|---|---|
| *Detect magic* | No magic at all where the ghost appeared. The chalk circle and the two scarecrows in the crypt glow with necromancy. |
| *Detect poison and disease* | Poison in Silas's body, residue in the tonic glass (C6), and a faint trace inside Quill's bag, where an empty vial rolls loose. |
| *Speak with dead* | Silas's five answers (Adventure, Act 2). |
| *Zone of truth* | Innocent suspects give up their secrets. Quill goes silent or speaks only in careful half-truths. |
| *Detect thoughts* | Surface thoughts: each suspect is fixated on their own secret. Quill is thinking "midnight... the brazier... don't look at the bag." Probing deeper, Quill makes a DC 13 Wisdom save (+4). |
| *Locate object* | If the caster saw the will in Scene 2, it points to Quill's bag. Let it work; the finale starts early. |
| *Augury* | "Search the doctor's bag": weal. "Accuse Edric": woe. |
| *Revivify* | Too late. Silas has been dead far longer than a minute. |
| Divine Sense | Senses desecrated ground at the crypt's chalk circle. |
| Familiar or *invisibility* to tail someone | Whoever they follow does exactly what the timeline says. Quill heads for the chapel at candle 11. |
| Insight against Quill | Contest it with his Deception (+5). On a win: "He's hiding something, and he's very good at it." |

## Keeping the secret

The twist lands when players suspect Quill late and then realize every clue was in plain sight. Five habits get you there:

1. **Make him useful.** Quill offers real help: he confirms the poison once someone else finds it, and he patches up wounds. Players rarely suspect the NPC who keeps helping them.
2. **Give every suspect equal time.** If you linger on Quill, players notice. Rotate the spotlight evenly through all six.
3. **Let him be wrong out loud.** His theories about the ghost, then Edric, then Morwenna should be plausible and confident. Players enjoy proving the doctor wrong, and it hides that he is steering them.
4. **Never lie about a clue.** Describe the mint smell, the buckled boots and the dark guest room plainly every time. Fairness is what makes the reveal satisfying.
5. **Save the mask.** Keep the plague doctor mask out of sight until the showdown, then put it on. Pull it off at the unmasking for the big moment. (In Foundry: Quill's token stays masked until the Unmask macro swaps it.)

## Voice cheat sheet

One hook per character is enough to keep six suspects distinct.

| Character | Voice and habit |
|---|---|
| Lord Silas | Thin, dry and amused; taps his raven cane for emphasis |
| Dr. Quill | Warm and slow, like a favorite uncle; pushes up his spectacles; "just trying to help" |
| Lady Morwenna | Clipped and cool; snaps her fan shut to end a conversation |
| Edric | Loud, slurred bluster; gestures with his glass and his papier-mâché head |
| Pip | Fast and breathless; pushes her hat out of her eyes; apologizes mid-sentence |
| Bramwell | Low gravel; long pauses; spits before anything important |
| Mrs. Crane | Crisp and precise; jingles her keys when she disapproves |
| Fennick | Fussy and squeaky; wipes his fogged spectacles constantly |
| The Widow | Silent. A slow bow of the head, nothing more |
