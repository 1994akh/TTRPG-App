Prologue-

Enkrid stooped behind the hovels edge, tightly hugging his knees to his chest, body aching from . Hidden by the darkness of the moonless night and the shadows cast by the communal fire at center of the small so called village, the small child shivered silent. The child did not shiver due to the warm humid air of the mid-summer night nor the soft dry breeze that rolled off of the grassy planes that stretched seemingly with no end. Fear drove him, his hear raced at every noise.

N the largest or tallest of the village’s children, nor did Enkrid ever expect he would be, his wiry frame showed only the smallest possibility of blooming into height or muscle, his greatest wish. Even while considering his long standing gripe against the gods, spirits or whomever had cursed him with his frame, he knew that his future size was the least of his concerns at the moment. His muscles ached from making himself small to hide, yet he still risked it. 

Paces away, people of all ages, seemingly the entire village sat, laid, or silently stood to listen to a lone man standing near the fire, central for all to see, around 30 people. Painted in by the flickering fire, the people of the village looked Erie in the fright Enkrid thought to himself. The orange of the small fire discoloring the villagers grey and green stone like skin gave each a nightmarish look, only quickening his heart in fear as he peered from his hiding place. 

The figure in the center gesticulated with one hand and rhythmically thumped his long walking staff in time with his words.
